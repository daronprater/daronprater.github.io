Hygiene & Exams

Proper dental hygiene, including regular professional cleanings, is the key to a healthy, happy smile. A hygiene appointment is more than cleaning your teeth — it's a complete oral wellness exam.

What to expect at your visit

  • Oral cancer screening
  • Periodontal risk assessment
  • Full dental charting
  • Complete cleaning & polishing
  • Personalized hygiene instructions
  • Oral product recommendations

Why it matters: your oral health is directly connected to your overall health. Untreated gum disease has been linked to heart disease, diabetes and more — so we look at the whole picture, not just your teeth.

Crowns & Bridges

A dental crown is a tooth-shaped shell that fits over the entire tooth, covering and protecting it. Each crown is made individually to blend beautifully with your other teeth — strong, durable and natural-looking.

Porcelain ceramic

Lifelike translucency for front and back teeth.

Bruxer zirconia

Exceptionally strong — ideal for patients who grind.

Implant crowns

The final, natural-looking piece of a dental implant.

Crowns can help with

  • Teeth worn down by grinding
  • Badly stained or oddly shaped teeth
  • Teeth weakened by a root canal
  • Cracked or broken teeth
  • Teeth damaged by decay
  • Replacing a missing tooth with an implant

Dental bridges

Bridges combine crowns and artificial teeth to replace one or more missing teeth. Crowns on the neighboring teeth (or dental implants) hold replacement teeth — called pontics — in the gap, restoring your smile and your ability to chew with ease.

Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers are thin shells of ceramic bonded directly to the front surfaces of your teeth. Once placed, they blend beautifully with your natural smile and are highly resistant to coffee and tea stains.

A transformed smile

Ideal for chipped, discolored or misshapen teeth.

Less invasive

Preserves more natural tooth than a traditional crown.

Stain resistant

Porcelain stays bright against coffee and tea.

Teeth Whitening

Don't let stains and discoloration dim your smile. Professional whitening safely lifts the stains left behind by coffee, tea and other everyday culprits.

  1. Consultation. Dr. Prater checks the health of your teeth and gums to confirm whitening is right for you.
  2. Custom trays. We take a mold of your teeth to create thin, comfortable trays made just for you.
  3. At-home whitening. You'll use your trays with professional whitening gel for about a month.
  4. Follow-up. A final visit with Dr. Prater to review and finalize your results.

Inlays & Onlays

When decay goes beyond what a filling can handle, porcelain inlays and onlays offer a stronger, longer-lasting option than fillings — and a more conservative one than crowns. Because they're bonded directly to your tooth and color-matched, they restore both strength and beauty.

Inlays

Fill the space between the cusps at the center of the tooth's biting surface.

Onlays

Work like inlays but extend over one or more cusps of the tooth.

The process

  1. Decay is gently removed from the tooth.
  2. An impression is taken so your custom inlay or onlay can be crafted.
  3. A temporary filling protects the tooth in the meantime.
  4. At your second visit, the porcelain restoration is permanently bonded in place.

Gum health

Periodontal Therapy

Gum disease is a silent condition that often begins without symptoms — many people have it and don't realize it. Left untreated, it harms not only your teeth and gums but your overall health. We offer modern, non-surgical periodontal treatment to stop it early.

  • Periodontal scaling — removing buildup below the gumline.
  • Root planing — smoothing root surfaces so gums can reattach and heal.
  • Arestin — localized antibiotic therapy placed directly where it's needed.
Comfort

TMJ Treatment

Temporomandibular joint disorder (TMJ) occurs when the joint connecting your jaw and skull stops working smoothly. Symptoms can include jaw pain, migraine headaches and clicking or popping. Treatment depends on the cause and intensity of your symptoms, and may include:

  • A custom mouth or bite guard
  • Bite adjustments to relieve pressure
  • Physical therapy
  • Prescription pain relief

Facial esthetics

Botox & Dermal Fillers

Dr. Prater is certified by the American Academy of Facial Esthetics to administer Botox and dermal filler treatments — a natural complement to a beautiful smile.

Botox

A simple, non-surgical treatment that temporarily smooths moderate to severe frown lines between the brows, as well as crow's feet and forehead lines. One quick, roughly 10-minute treatment can show noticeable improvement within days, lasting up to four months. Dr. Prater examines how you move the muscles of your brow, then places several tiny injections precisely where they're needed.

Dermal fillers (Juvéderm)

Juvéderm is a smooth gel made of hyaluronic acid, a substance that occurs naturally in your skin and helps it stay hydrated and full. Injected beneath the skin, it instantly restores volume and smooths wrinkles and folds — no allergy testing required.

Juvéderm Ultra

Versatile contouring and volume for wrinkles and folds.

Juvéderm Ultra Plus

A more robust formula for deeper folds and wrinkles.
