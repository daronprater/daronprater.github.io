First visit

What to expect

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Your completed patient forms, a photo ID, your dental insurance card (if you have one) and a list of any medications you take.

How do I schedule an appointment?

Call our office at (513) 793-3722 during business hours and our team will find a time that works for you.

Do you see children?

Absolutely — we love seeing kids. We recommend a child's first dental visit at about age two.

Where do I park?

Our office is at 8040 Hosbrook Road, Suite 230, across from the Kenwood Tower off I-71, with a parking lot right outside the building entrance.
