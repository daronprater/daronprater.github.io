Why implants

Looks, feels and works like your own tooth.

An implant is a small titanium post that serves as a new tooth root. Topped with a custom crown, it restores your bite without relying on neighboring teeth for support — the closest thing to a natural tooth modern dentistry offers.

Natural look

Custom crowns are color-matched to blend with your smile.

Protects bone

Helps preserve the jaw bone that weakens after tooth loss.

Spares healthy teeth

No need to reshape neighboring teeth for support.

Built to last

A stable, long-term solution you can eat and smile with confidently.

The process

Your implant journey, step by step.

Each phase is carefully planned and performed by Dr. Prater, with plenty of healing time built in for results that last.

01

Tooth extraction & bone grafting

Extraction

The first step is removing the tooth where the implant will go, leaving an opening in the bone that once supported it. On its own, that bone won't fully fill in as the area heals — weakening support for your future implant and the teeth beside it.

Bone grafting

Right after the extraction, a bone graft is placed to encourage new, healthy bone to form in the site. The area is closed and allowed to heal while the graft "takes." Once the new bone is dense enough, you're ready for your implant.

02

Implant placement

Dr. Prater makes a small incision in the gum to expose the bone, prepares the site, and places the implant deep in the bone where it will act as your new tooth root. The incision is then closed and healing begins.

Over the next several months, the implant integrates with your natural bone — creating a foundation as strong as the real thing.

03

Abutment & implant crown

Impressions of your teeth are used to craft a custom implant crown. Once your gums have healed and the implant has fully integrated, Dr. Prater places your new crown — completing a seamless, natural-looking smile.

✦

Post-operative care

Some discomfort is normal after each phase. You'll leave every visit with clear instructions, including:

  • Any medications you should take
  • Best practices for keeping the site healthy
  • What to eat — and avoid — while you heal

Full-arch implants

All-on-4® & All-on-6: a complete smile, anchored for good.

Missing most of your teeth, facing extractions, or tired of loose dentures? A full arch of fixed, natural-looking teeth can be secured on just four or six implants. They don't come out at night and don't cover the roof of your mouth.

4

All-on-4®

Four implants support a full arch of teeth. The two back implants are placed at an angle to make the most of the denser bone toward the front of the jaw, which can reduce the need for bone grafting.

  • Often a fit for patients with some bone loss
  • Fewer implants, simpler surgery
6

All-on-6

Six implants spread across the arch share the load of chewing, giving the new teeth even more support and stability.

  • Often a fit for patients with good bone volume
  • Extra support for a strong bite

Is a full-arch implant right for you?

It may be a great option if you:

  • Are missing all or most of your upper or lower teeth
  • Have several failing teeth that can't be saved
  • Wear dentures that slip, rub or make eating difficult
  • Want fixed teeth that look, feel and function like your own

Which option fits best depends on your bone, bite and health. Dr. Prater will walk you through it using detailed imaging at your consultation.

How it works

  1. Consultation & imaging. We evaluate your bone and bite and plan exactly where each implant will go.
  2. Implant day. Any remaining failing teeth are removed and the implants are placed. When the bone allows, a set of temporary teeth can be attached the same day.
  3. Healing. Over the next few months the implants fuse with your jaw bone.
  4. Your final smile. A custom, permanent full-arch bridge is crafted and secured to the implants.

Common questions

Implant FAQ

How long does the implant process take?

It depends on your situation. Healing after a bone graft and integration of the implant each take time — often several months in total. Dr. Prater will give you a personalized timeline at your consultation.

Do I need a bone graft?

If a tooth is being extracted, a graft is usually placed right away to preserve and rebuild the bone so your implant has a solid foundation. Dr. Prater will let you know what's right for you.

What's the difference between All-on-4® and All-on-6?

Both replace a full arch of teeth with a fixed bridge anchored to implants. All-on-4® uses four implants, with the back two angled to use the jaw's denser front bone, which can reduce the need for grafting. All-on-6 uses six implants for extra support. Dr. Prater will recommend the best option based on your bone and bite.

I already wear dentures. Can I switch to full-arch implants?

Many denture wearers are good candidates. Fixed implant teeth don't slip, don't need adhesives and let you eat the foods you love again. A consultation and imaging will show whether it's right for you.

Is the procedure done in your office?

Yes. Extraction, grafting, implant placement and the final crown are all performed by Dr. Prater right here in our Kenwood office.
