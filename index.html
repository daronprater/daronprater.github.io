Implant dentistry

Replace missing teeth for good.

Whether it's a dental emergency or you're looking for a more effective long-term solution, a dental implant looks, feels and functions just like a natural tooth — and protects the healthy teeth around it.

See the implant process
01

Extraction & grafting

We gently remove the damaged tooth and graft the site to build a strong, healthy foundation of bone.

02

Implant placement

Dr. Prater places the implant deep in the bone, where it fuses over several months to become your new root.

03

Your new crown

A custom implant crown is crafted from impressions of your teeth and secured for a seamless, lasting smile.
